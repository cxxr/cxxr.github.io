📖 Game Manual & Business Math Guide

Welcome to Hot Dog Stand: The Works!

In this simulation, you take on the role of an entrepreneur running a hot dog stand across 8 major community and sporting events. Your mission is to reach your season profit target by making smart math and business choices!

1. The Weather & Frankie's Forecast

Before every event, check the forecast with Frankie the Weather Wiener:

  • Blazing Heatwave: Cold drinks (soda & lemonade) sell at double the normal rate! Hot food demand drops slightly.
  • Chilly & Brisk: Hot cocoa and warm cider sell heavily, while cold drinks drop by 50%.
  • Rain or Storms: Crowd size shrinks by 25% to 45%. Don't overbuy perishable supplies!

2. Managing Supplies & Perishability

Packaging in the real world doesn't always line up 1-to-1:

  • Hot Dogs: Come in packs of 10, cartons of 50, or wholesale crates of 100. Refrigerated (lasts 2 events).
  • Bakery Buns: Come in bags of 8, trays of 48, or bins of 96. Highly perishable: fresh buns spoil after 1 event!
  • Condiments, Drinks, and Chips: Shelf-stable and roll over into future events.

3. Price Elasticity of Demand

You can set any price you want, but customers respond to value:

  • Fair Price (~$2.75 for a hot dog): Healthy demand and steady sales.
  • Discounted Price: Earns customer appreciation and slightly higher volume.
  • Overpriced ($4.00+): Customers will cry "Highway robbery!" and refuse to buy, leaving you with unsold food and wasted expenses.

4. The Accounting Income Statement

At the end of each day, review your financial statement:

Gross Revenue (Total Sales)
− Cost of Goods Sold (Supplies actually used)
= Gross Profit
− Operating Expenses (Advertising + Stand Permit)
− Spoilage Loss (Perishable food that went bad)
= Net Profit (or Loss!)

5. In-Game Calculator

Click the 🧮 Calculator button anytime to calculate bun-to-dog ratios or compute your profit margin percentage: (Price − Cost) ÷ Price × 100.
